Guide

MobileWebCamera · Android 9 or later

It takes three steps. Connect your phone and PC by USB or Wi-Fi → tap “Start streaming” in the app → paste the URL it shows into an OBS “Media Source”.

On this page

1. Connect your phone and PC

Put your phone and PC on the same network in one of these two ways.

USB (recommended)

The most stable option with the lowest latency. You don't need adb or any developer tools.

  1. Connect the phone to the PC with a USB cableUse a cable that supports data, not a charge-only cable.
  2. Turn on USB tetheringOn the phone, open Settings → Network & internet → Hotspot & tethering → USB tethering. Menu names vary slightly by phone.

Wi-Fi

Connect the phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi network (the same router). No cable needed, but the video may stutter if the signal is weak.

Mobile data (4G / 5G) won't work

The app only shows USB tethering and Wi-Fi addresses, because those are the only ones your PC can reach.

2. Start streaming

  1. Open the app and allow camera access
  2. On the Stream tab, tap “Start streaming”On Android 17 and later you'll be asked to allow “local network access”. Allow it — your PC can't connect without it.
  3. Check the URL that appearsYou'll see a URL like tcp://192.168.42.129:4747, labeled USB or Wi-Fi. You'll use it in the next step.

Streaming continues even if you leave the app. While the “streaming” notification is showing, the stream is live. To stop, go back to the app and tap “Stop streaming”.

3. Receive in OBS

  1. In OBS, click “+” under Sources and choose “Media Source”
  2. Use these settings, then click “OK”
SettingValue
Local FileOff
InputThe URL shown in the app (e.g. tcp://192.168.42.129:4747)
Input Formatmpegts
Network Buffering0 MB
FFmpeg Optionsanalyzeduration=500000
Use hardware decoding when availableOn

FFmpeg Options is optional, but it cuts the time until video appears from about 5 seconds to about 1.5 seconds.

Using it in Zoom, Teams and other apps

Click “Start Virtual Camera” in OBS. The OBS output then shows up as a webcam you can pick in other apps.

4. Receive without OBS

You can also change tcp:// to http://. In VLC, open Media → Open Network Stream and enter:

http://192.168.42.129:4747/

Replace the IP address with the one shown in the app.

5. What each tab does

Open the settings from the menu button at the top right. The app only shows options your phone's camera supports, so what you see depends on the device.

Video orientation

By default it's set to “Auto”, which keeps the video upright however you hold the phone. If you lay the phone flat, it keeps the last orientation. When the phone is on a stand, locking the orientation keeps things steady.

  • Portrait: streams a tall video (e.g. 1080×1920)
  • Landscape (right side up) / (left side up): with the screen facing you, the right or left edge of the phone is on top

“Multi” and “Physical” lenses

Multi is a camera that switches lenses automatically as you zoom. Physical uses one lens directly. On phones that can't output from a single physical lens, the app falls back to Multi automatically — pick the lens with the zoom level instead.

6. Troubleshooting

Nothing shows up in OBS / can't connect
  • Make sure you've tapped “Start streaming” in the app.
  • Make sure the phone and PC are on the same network (USB tethering, or the same Wi-Fi).
  • Check that the OBS “Input” matches the URL the app shows right now. The IP address can change when you reconnect USB.
  • On Android 17 and later, allow “local network access”. If you denied it, change it in Settings → Apps → MobileWebCamera → Permissions.
  • A PC firewall, or office, school or public Wi-Fi, may block the connection. Use USB in that case.
Video takes a long time to appear / high latency
  • Set OBS “Network Buffering” to 0 MB.
  • Put analyzeduration=500000 in “FFmpeg Options”.
  • USB has lower latency than Wi-Fi.
Video is glitchy or blocky
  • Tap “Request keyframe” on the Stream tab to resend a clean frame.
  • Lower the bitrate or resolution.
  • On Wi-Fi, move closer to the router or switch to USB.
Video is sideways or upside down

On the Stream tab, set “Video orientation” to match how you hold the phone.

“Cannot open camera” or “Camera disconnected”

Another app may be using the camera. Close other camera apps and try again.

The phone gets hot or the battery drains fast

Long streams can make the phone warm. Try a lower resolution or frame rate, remove the case, or connect by USB so it charges while streaming.
