Your phone camera,
now a PC webcam.

Stream your Android phone's camera to your PC over USB tethering or Wi-Fi.
Receive it with an OBS “Media Source” — nothing extra to install on the PC.

Read the guide GitHub

Features

Nothing to install on the PC

Receive it in OBS or VLC on Windows, macOS or Linux.

Low-latency H.264

Hardware-encoded to H.264 on the phone and sent as MPEG-TS.

Full camera controls

Switch lenses, manual focus, ISO, shutter speed, white balance and more.

Your data stays with you

Video goes straight to the PC you connect. No account, no ads, no analytics.

What you need

  • An Android phone with a camera, running Android 9 or later
  • A PC with OBS Studio or another receiver
  • A USB cable, or the same Wi-Fi network for phone and PC
