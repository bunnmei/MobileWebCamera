Privacy Policy

MobileWebCamera · Effective and last updated: October 16, 2026

This app does not collect any personal information. Camera video is sent only, and directly, to the devices you connect yourself, such as your PC. It is never sent to the developer or to any outside server.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app “MobileWebCamera” (the “App”), provided by bunnmei (the “developer”), handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect any user information through the App. Specifically:

  • There is no account sign-up or login.
  • The App does not access your name, email address, location, contacts or similar information.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other SDKs that send data elsewhere.
  • The developer runs no servers, and the App never communicates with the developer.

2. How camera video is handled

  • Video is encoded to H.264 on your device, only while you have streaming turned on (“Start streaming”).
  • The encoded video is sent directly to devices that connect to the App over USB tethering or the same Wi-Fi network — for example, a PC running OBS or VLC. It does not pass through any server on the internet.
  • The App does not save photos or videos to your device. If you want a recording, make it in the receiving software (such as OBS).
  • The App does not use the microphone.
Who can see the stream

While streaming, any device on the same network can receive the video by connecting to the URL the App shows. The video stream is not password-protected or encrypted.

Use the App on a network you trust, such as your home network, or over USB tethering, and tap “Stop streaming” when you're done. We don't recommend using it on public Wi-Fi.

3. Permissions and why they're needed

PermissionWhy it's needed
CameraTo capture video
InternetTo accept connections from your PC and send video to it (Android requires this permission for any network communication; the App does not connect to servers on the internet)
Network / Wi-Fi stateTo show the addresses (IP addresses) your PC can connect to
Local network accessOn Android 17 and later, to accept connections from a PC on the same network
Foreground service (camera)To keep streaming after you leave the App
Prevent sleepTo keep the device from sleeping and stopping the video while streaming
NotificationsTo show that streaming is on in the notification shade

Anything obtained through these permissions is used on your device only for the purposes above, and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the developer holds no user information, none is shared with third parties.

If you got the App from Google Play, Google handles information such as downloads under its own privacy policy. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

5. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App's features change. When we do, we'll update this page and the “last updated” date above.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us through GitHub Issues.

The App's source code is available on GitHub.
